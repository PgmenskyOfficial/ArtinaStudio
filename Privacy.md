## Crash Reporting and Diagnostics

ArtinaStudio includes a built-in Crash Reporter designed to help diagnose and fix unexpected application errors quickly. 

### What Data We Collect
In the event of an application crash or critical error, the Crash Reporter may collect and log the following diagnostic information:
* **System Specifications:** Details regarding your system's hardware and environment, including processor (CPU) specs, total and available system RAM, and operating system version.
* **Error Logs:** Technical stack traces, exception details, and log entries generated leading up to the application failure.

### Privacy Assurance
We only care about purely understanding the technical nature of the bug to fix it. **We do not want, collect, or look at your personal files, private documents, or custom code.** The diagnostic data is used strictly for technical troubleshooting and improving ArtinaStudio's stability.
